# Product

## What it is

KBC Weerbericht: a concept feature for KBC Mobile, built by Team Trojan Horse for the KBC challenge
of the Tectonic hackathon (Kortrijk). It turns a personal cash-flow forecast into a weather report:
sun when a day is calm, rain when a planned payment lands, thunder after an impulse night out,
storm when a purchase would push the account into the red. Kate, KBC's assistant, reshuffles the
week and warns before it goes wrong. The same "weather" language carries a fraud warning
("code rood") for older customers.

## Surfaces

- `KBC-Forecast/`: Expo app, clickable demo with five scenes.
- `website/`: 3D pitch landing page for jury and presentation. Mode: Persuade.

## Audience

Hackathon jury (KBC people, tech and business), then peers. They see it on a projector and on
laptops; some open it on a phone.

## Personas (fictional)

- Lotte, 23, first job, saves for Lisbon, is tempted by a new phone.
- Jos, 74, is called by a fake "KBC security service" and asked to transfer EUR 2.450.
- Els, Jos' daughter, his guardian angel contact.

## Voice

Dutch (Flemish), warm, direct, no blame ("Geen verwijt"). Weather metaphors, never jargon.

## Constraints

- Not an official KBC product. Always show the disclaimer: concept mockup, fictional people and
  amounts.
- Look follows the KBC Mobile dark theme used in the app (#111315 background, #1A9FE4 blue,
  Nunito Sans standing in for MuseoSans).
